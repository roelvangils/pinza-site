What's coming

These features are planned but not in the app yet. They're listed here so the rest of this documentation can stay strictly about what works today. All of them will be part of Pinza Pro, at no extra cost to existing license holders.

  • Groups — organize pins into named groups and filter by them everywhere.
  • Tags & labels — a second, looser axis for organizing pins.
  • Advanced search & filters — search by app, site, date, and type.
  • Shortcuts integration — Pinza actions as building blocks in Apple Shortcuts.
  • iCloud sync — your pins on all your Macs.
  • Spotlight integration — find pins from the system-wide search.

No dates. Small teams that promise dates break them.

If one of these matters to you, say so — it genuinely affects what gets built first.
