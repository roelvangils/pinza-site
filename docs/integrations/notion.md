# Notion

Connected to Notion, the Copy as… menu on any Notion page gains **AI-Ready Markdown** — the whole page as clean Markdown, ready for an AI assistant, a README, or anywhere else.

## Setup

1. Open **Settings → Integrations → Notion**.
2. Click **Sign in with Notion** and pick your workspace in the browser window that opens.

## Share the pages you want to copy

Notion only lets an integration read pages you've explicitly shared with it. For each page (children inherit it):

1. Open the page in Notion and click **•••** in the top-right corner.
2. Choose **Connections** and add **Pinza**.

{% shot "Notion's ••• menu open on a page with Connections expanded and Pinza being added — the one step people miss.", "menu" %}

If you copy a page you haven't shared yet, Pinza tells you exactly that instead of failing silently.

## Using it

1. With the page open — in the Notion app or in a browser — press your Copy shortcut.
2. Hover the HUD, open **Copy as…**, and choose **AI-Ready Markdown**.
3. Paste.
